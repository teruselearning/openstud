🌿 Open Source  ·  Free to use  ·  Self-hosted

Professional collection management
for wildlife & botanical organisations

OpenStudbook is a self-hosted platform built for zoos, wildlife sanctuaries, aquariums, botanical gardens, and conservation programmes. Track individual animals and plants, manage breeding records, monitor genetics, and collaborate with partner organisations — all in one place.

🐆

Fauna Management

Register and track individual animals. Record sex, lineage, weight history, health logs, and GPS location. Generate breeding recommendations based on genetic diversity.

🌳

Flora & Botanical Records

Document trees, shrubs, and plant specimens with studbook IDs, planting dates, location mapping, and observation history. Supports monoecious and dioecious classification.

🧬

Genetics & Studbooks

Maintain official studbook records with sire and dam lineage. Track DNA sequences and assess inbreeding coefficients to guide conservation breeding decisions.

🗺️

Live Location Mapping

Pin individuals on an interactive map with high-accuracy GPS. View your entire collection spatially, with real-time location tracking on mobile devices.

🤝

Partner Network

Connect with other organisations running OpenStudbook. Propose and manage breeding loans, share species data, and coordinate conservation efforts globally.

🔒

Private & Secure

Your data lives on your own server. Granular privacy controls let you decide exactly what to share publicly, what stays within your organisation, and what is visible to partners only.

Open Source  ·  Free to use  ·  Self-hosted

Professional collection management
for wildlife & botanical organisations

OpenStudbook is a self-hosted platform built for zoos, wildlife sanctuaries, aquariums, botanical gardens, and conservation programmes. Track individual animals and plants, manage breeding records, monitor genetics, and collaborate with partner organisations — all in one place.

Individual Records

Register every animal or plant with a studbook ID, name, sex, birth date, weight history, microchip number, and origin. Filter, search, and export your entire collection in seconds.

Species Catalog

Maintain detailed species profiles with scientific names, conservation status, life expectancy, and breeding seasons. Each species gets AI-enriched data with native status information.

Live Location Mapping

Pin individuals on an interactive map with high-accuracy GPS. View your entire collection spatially and track real-time location from mobile devices in the field.

Enclosures & Areas

Define enclosure boundaries on a map, assign individuals, and manage feed schedules with ingredient tracking. A monthly shopping list is computed automatically from active diets.

Dashboard Analytics

At-a-glance overview of your collection: species counts, sex ratios, origin breakdown (wild-caught vs captive-bred), and per-species population — all updated in real time.

Partner Network

Connect with other organisations via invite codes. Browse a global map of partners, view public species lists, and message organisations directly about collaboration opportunities.

Breeding Recommendations

The system automatically identifies unrelated male-female pairs within each species and ranks them as high-potential matches, helping you maximise genetic diversity.

Breeding Events

Record mating events, pregnancies, births, and parentage. Track lineage through sire and dam fields, building a complete genetic history for every individual in your collection.

Breeding Loan Management

Propose, negotiate, and track breeding loans between partner organisations. Set durations and terms, send loan requests, and monitor the status of animals on loan — all within the platform.

Multi-Language & Projects

Full internationalisation with AI-powered translations. Organise your collection into separate projects — by location, taxon group, or programme — under a single organisation.

AI-Powered Tools

Bring your own Gemini or OpenRouter key for unlimited AI features: auto-create species profiles, generate images, enrich CSV imports, and translate the entire interface.

Private & Secure

Your data lives on your own server. Granular privacy controls let you decide what to share publicly, what stays within your organisation, and what is visible to partners only.
